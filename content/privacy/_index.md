The OpenEMR Foundation, Inc. is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit that supports the OpenEMR open-source project. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we and the services we rely on collect when you use **www.open-emr.org**, why, and what choices you have.

The short version: we don't sell your information, we don't run advertising, and the only optional tracking on this site is Google Analytics, which stays off unless you click **Accept**.

## What this policy covers

This policy covers www.open-emr.org, including the project wiki at www.open-emr.org/wiki/.

It does **not** cover these related sites, which are run separately:

- **The community forum** at community.open-emr.org
- **The online demos** at demo.openemr.io. These are public test systems that reset every day. Never enter real patient information into a demo.
- **Sites we link to**, including donation platforms, GitHub, OpenCoreEMR and other partners. Once you leave our site, that site's own privacy policy applies.

OpenEMR is software that clinics install and run themselves. The Foundation does not host anyone's medical records and never receives patient information through this website. Please don't post health information about yourself or anyone else on the wiki.

## Information collected when you visit

### Server and network logs

Like nearly every website, ours is delivered through several infrastructure services. Each one automatically receives the technical details your browser sends with every request: your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, the referring page, and your browser and device type.

- **Cloudflare** sits in front of the site to speed it up and block attacks.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts most of the site's pages.
- **DigitalOcean** hosts the server that runs the wiki and routes traffic to GitHub Pages. That server keeps standard web access logs.

We use these logs only to keep the site running, secure and free of abuse. We keep our own server logs only as long as needed for those purposes, and we don't use them to profile individual visitors.

### Analytics (only if you accept)

If you click **Accept** on the cookie banner, we use **Google Analytics 4** to understand which pages are useful and how people find us. Google Analytics then sets cookies and collects information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location (city or country level), your browser and device type, and the site that referred you.

We've configured it to collect as little as practical:

- Google signals and ad personalization are turned off, so this data isn't used for advertising.
- Data is kept for 2 months and then deleted.
- According to Google, Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.

If you click **Decline**, or never click either button, Google Analytics is not loaded at all. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.

### Content loaded from other services

To display the site, your browser fetches some files directly from other providers. That means those providers receive your IP address and browser details:

- **Fonts** from Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts
- **Code libraries** from jsDelivr, cdnjs (Cloudflare), the Bootstrap CDN and the jQuery CDN
- **Videos** embedded from YouTube on the homepage, the lectures pages and a few blog posts. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, so YouTube doesn't set cookies unless you play a video.

### Search engine data

We use Google Search Console to see how the site appears in Google search results. It reports totals from Google's side, like search terms and click counts, and doesn't collect anything from you on our site.

## Information you choose to give us

### Wiki accounts

If you create a wiki account, we store your username, your password (in a scrambled, hashed form) and your email address if you provide one. When you're logged in, the wiki uses cookies to keep you signed in. Edits are public: the page history shows your username next to each change. If you edit without logging in, your IP address is shown publicly in its place.

### Donations

Donations are handled by **Zeffy**, **GitHub Sponsors** and **Open Collective**. You give your payment details directly to those platforms, and the Foundation never sees or stores card numbers. The platform shares with us what's needed to thank you, issue receipts and keep nonprofit records, such as your name, email address, the amount and date, and any message you include. Each platform's own privacy policy also applies.

### Email updates

If you sign up for our email updates, we use Mailchimp to send them. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe.

## Cookies

| Cookie | Set by | Purpose | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| `__cf_bm`, `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Security: tells real visitors apart from bots | May be set on any visit |
| Wiki session cookies | Our wiki (MediaWiki) | Keeps you logged in | Only if you log in to the wiki |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Google Analytics | Analytics | Only after you click **Accept** |

Your consent choice is saved in your browser's local storage (not a cookie) so the banner doesn't reappear on every page. The site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

## How we use and share information

We use information only to run, secure and improve the site and the OpenEMR project, to process and acknowledge donations, and to send updates you asked for.

**We do not sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.** We share it only with the service providers named in this policy, so they can do their jobs for us, or when the law requires it.

## Where information is processed

The Foundation and most of our service providers are based in the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, including the EU, UK, India or Nigeria, your information will be processed in the United States and in other countries where those providers operate.

## Your choices and rights

- **Analytics:** decline on the banner, or change your choice anytime with **Cookie settings**.
- **Email:** use the unsubscribe link in any email.
- **Wiki:** you can update your account settings. For other requests, contact us.
- **Access, correction and deletion:** you can ask what personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Public wiki history and records we're legally required to keep, such as donation records, may be exceptions.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the EU and UK GDPR, U.S. state privacy laws, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or the Nigeria Data Protection Act. These can include the right to object to processing, to withdraw consent, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Contact us and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

## Children

This site is meant for people who work with or develop OpenEMR. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

We use HTTPS across the site and rely on reputable infrastructure providers. No website can guarantee perfect security, but we limit what we collect so there's less to protect.

## Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. This website is open source, so every past version of this policy is public in our [website's GitHub repository](https://github.com/openemr/website-openemr/commits/master/content/privacy/_index.md).
